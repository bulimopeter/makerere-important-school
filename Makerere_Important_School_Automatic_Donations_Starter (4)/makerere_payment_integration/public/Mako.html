Our Commitment —
No Child Left Behind

We are committed to ensuring every child receives access to quality basic education regardless of their family's financial situation. Through community support and generosity, we keep hope and learning alive for the children of Kawaala. Both Airtel Money and MTN Mobile Money donations are welcome.
